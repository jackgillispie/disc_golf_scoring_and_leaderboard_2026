@Lake Norman IT Professionals hosts a flagship disc golf tournament each year with over 200 attendees. 
A key challenge is maximizing sponsor ROI. Why should a company attend, let alone contribute? 

This is a unique opportunity for business leaders to:

- Develop a personal network of experts who inform their strategy
- Identify and develop relationships with new customers at low-stakes
- Demonstrate commitment to serving the community
- Advertise business services/products to the community

Our app secures several short-term wins: 

- Continuous, unavoidable sponsor impressions in front of high-value attendees
- Enhanced reputation of host and sponsors
- Effortless player experience

In the long run, we:
 
- Attract new sponsors and organizations
- Highlight the relevance of automation and security
- Provide ongoing projects for early professionals to contribute to

𝗕𝘂𝗶𝗹𝗱𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗭𝗲𝗿𝗼 𝗗𝗼𝘄𝗻𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗲 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗭𝗲𝗿𝗼 𝗗𝗼𝗹𝗹𝗮𝗿𝘀

Our Claude-assisted visual draft required adaptions to become resilient and cost-aware. Consider:

- Disc golf courses are notorious for spotty cellular coverage. Vendor logos must remain visible. 
- Netlify limits credits for requests and images. We cannot exceed those limits.
- Scores may be sent in 'bursts', which can lead to failed submissions.  

So we adapted. We handled network outages by making the app retry intelligently. We used Wix to keep logos visible for free and cached images on startup.

To validate this design, I authored a Python script to simulate network outages and burst traffic, and tested at the game site. This confirmed our solution worked in the real world.

𝗦𝗲𝗰𝘂𝗿𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗦𝗽𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗼𝗿𝘀𝗵𝗶𝗽

Hosting an event for IT / security professionals means a technical failure or security oversight damages the reputation of the host and our sponsors.

That's why we conducted a risk assessment and remediated attack chains that prevent the app from serving its purpose: showcasing our sponsors and submitting scores. 

We implemented alphanumeric whitelists on the frontend and backend. This preserves app integrity against XSS / formula injections.

We stopped unauthorized application modification by hardening repository and account permissions. 

We designed the backend so scores were not overwritten automatically. This includes an immutable audit trail and private score adjustment utilities, allowing formal score changes without disruption.
